Sunday Rat helps football fans put their phones away and enjoy the match. Fans choose their club, find a fixture, and activate Fan Mode to record phone-free participation and earn points during the game.

The project brings together an Expo React Native app, a Fastify API, and a React marketing website available in English and Greek. The mobile experience adapts to each club’s colours, while the backend handles authentication, fixtures, session syncing, and scoring with PostgreSQL and Drizzle.

Participation is recorded locally after check-in and synced when connectivity returns. The app is currently in beta for iOS and Android.
