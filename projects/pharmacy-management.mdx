A pharmacy management application that brings customer records, account balances, and prescription tracking into one workspace. Its Greek-language interface supports the daily workflow from checking outstanding balances to finding upcoming prescriptions.

### Daily overview

The dashboard shows customer totals, the overall account balance, pending prescriptions for the month, and prescriptions scheduled for today. A calendar provides a monthly view of prescription activity.

### Customers and prescriptions

Customer records include transaction history with debit and credit entries. Prescriptions can be searched and tracked by date, doctor, supplies, paperless status, barcode, and completion state. Barcode lookup connects scanned or typed codes to a local catalogue of Greek medicines.

### Built with

React, TanStack Start, Router, Query, Form, and Table, with PostgreSQL and Prisma. Shared components and CSS Modules support light and dark themes.
